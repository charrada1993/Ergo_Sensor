Setup & Alignment Guide

1

Position the Patient

Ensure the patient stands in the standard upright posture. Feet shoulder-width apart, spine aligned, arms hanging naturally at sides, gaze forward. This sets the 0° neutral reference.

NEUTRAL SCAN ALIGNMENT [0° REF]
2

Verify Anatomical Placement

Affix the wireless sensors securely to these clinical landmarks:

  • Trunk: Upper thoracic spine (near T1).
  • Upper Arm (L/R): Lateral aspect of the humerus.
  • Forearm (L/R): Dorsal aspect of the radius.
  • Wrist/Hand (L/R): Dorsal aspect of the metacarpus.
  • Neck: Cervical spine area (C3-C5).
  • Knee/Thigh (L/R): Lateral aspect of the femur.
3

Lock Calibration Baseline

Confirm the patient is motionless in the neutral pose, then trigger Capture Neutral Pose. This stores reference orientations to compute anatomical angles.

Place Your Sensors
